FAQs
Questions worth asking before your treatment.
These answers provide general information. Your consultation determines what is appropriate for you.
Anti-wrinkle injections vs dermal fillers, what's the difference?
Anti-wrinkle injections address selected movement-related lines by reducing targeted muscle activity, while dermal fillers are generally used to restore volume, structure and proportion. Some concerns need neither treatment, or a combination of both. The doctor will guide you after assessment.
How much filler do I need for my cheeks?
There's no standard amount. The quantity is determined after assessing your facial anatomy, existing volume, skin quality and desired outcome, not by a fixed package or a number you request in advance.
Do fillers look natural?
That depends heavily on the technique, quantity and the individual planning it. The approach here is anatomy-led and conservative by design, aimed at balanced, natural-looking outcomes rather than dramatic or immediately obvious change. No specific outcome can be guaranteed.
Can I request a particular number of millilitres?
You may discuss your preferences, but the product, quantity and areas are determined by the treating doctor based on anatomy, safety and the intended outcome.
Will I look overfilled or expressionless?
The treatment philosophy is conservative and individualised. No outcome can be guaranteed, but the plan is designed to preserve identity and appropriate expression.
How much do anti-wrinkle injections cost in Hyderabad?
Cost depends on the areas treated, the product used and the quantity your doctor recommends after assessment, so it isn't set in advance from a price list. Your consultation will include a clear breakdown once a plan has been discussed.
What are the side effects of dermal fillers and anti-wrinkle injections?
Swelling, redness, tenderness and bruising may occur. Other risks vary by treatment and will be discussed during consent. Seek urgent medical advice for any unexpected or concerning symptoms.
Is IV therapy safe after filler treatment?
It's assessed on an individual basis rather than assumed safe by default. The complimentary IV benefit requires its own separate medical assessment, covering allergies, medical history, and any recent procedures, before it's scheduled, whether that's the same day as your filler treatment or separately.
Is the complimentary IV therapy compulsory?
No. It is optional and subject to separate medical suitability. Declining it does not affect the quality of your injectable treatment.
Can the IV benefit be exchanged for a discount?
No. Complimentary benefits cannot be exchanged for cash, products, additional injectable quantity or a price reduction.
